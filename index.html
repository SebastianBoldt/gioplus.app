01 Measurement

Your iPhone as a goniometer

A physiotherapist reads the angle off a goniometer. Gio+ reads it from the camera image.

  1. 01

    Set up your iPhone

    Lean it against something and step back 1 to 2 m. Once the device is upright and your joint is in view from the side, the measurement starts by itself.

  2. 02

    Straighten and bend three times

    Hold each end position for 2 seconds. Voice prompts tell you what comes next.

  3. 03

    Check the result

    Gio+ saves the average of the three rounds, with a photo of each end position. You can also enter values by hand.

03 Who it is for

Injuries and operations where measuring pays off

After many injuries and operations the joint is stiff at first. Gio+ helps wherever flexion and extension are measured again and again over weeks.

Elbow

  • Elbow fracture: radial head, olecranon, distal humerus
  • Elbow dislocation
  • Ligament tear and ligament surgery
  • Stiff elbow after a cast or immobilisation
  • After arthrolysis

Knee

  • ACL tear and ACL reconstruction
  • Collateral ligament injury (MCL, LCL)
  • Meniscus tear and meniscus surgery
  • Knee replacement
  • Patella fracture, tibial plateau fracture
  • Arthrofibrosis

Shoulder

  • Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • Rotator cuff tear and rotator cuff repair
  • Proximal humerus fracture
  • Shoulder dislocation
  • Shoulder replacement

Gio+ measures raising the arm to the front.

Notice

Gio+ only measures. How far you may move the joint is up to your doctor or physiotherapist, especially in the first weeks after an operation.

04 Features

38 movements, four ways to measure

The camera works well for large joints seen from the side. For rotations, the spine, the wrist or the ankle, hold your iPhone against the body part: its motion sensor reads the angle like an inclinometer.

Every sensor measurement has its own animated guide that shows the position, where the iPhone goes and how to move. Voice prompts lead through three repetitions, so you never need to look at the screen. Movements through the neutral position are also shown in neutral-zero notation, for example 80–0–70.

05 Privacy

Where your data is stored

Your measurements are health data. They stay on your iPhone and, if you want, in your private iCloud.

Local

On your device

Measurements, photos and profiles are stored on your iPhone. Gio+ works offline.

Sync

In your iCloud

Your devices sync through your private iCloud. You can switch that off.

No server

No account, no server

Gio+ sends no data to any server. Camera images are analysed on the device.
