Measure with the camera

Three reps, one average

Prop up your iPhone, stand sideways and bend and straighten the joint three times. Gio+ detects the angle in the camera image and saves the average.

A measurement in under a minute.

Your first measurement is your baseline. Every later one is compared with it.

1

Set up your iPhone

Lean it against something or use a stand. Pick the joint and the side you want to measure.

2

Stand sideways

Bend and straighten the joint three times. Gio+ follows the movement in the camera image.

3

Check your result

Flexion, extension and range are saved as the average of your reps. You can also enter values by hand.

Know what you need.
Not just what you have.

Most everyday tasks don't need the full range of a healthy joint. Gio+ shows your current arc next to the functional range, so you can see at a glance how many degrees are still missing for bending and for straightening.

  • Flexion, extension and total range
  • Separate history for each joint and side
  • Photo history: compare start and end positions

Your health data stays yours.

Measurements say a lot about your body. That's why Gio+ keeps them where only you can reach them.

On your device

Measurements, photos and profiles live in a database on your iPhone. Gio+ works offline, too.

In your iCloud

Your devices sync through your private iCloud. You can turn that off in the settings at any time.

No account, no server

Gio+ doesn't send data to its own or anyone else's servers. Camera images are analysed right on the device.
