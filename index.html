01 Features

Your device as a goniometer

A physiotherapist reads the angle off a goniometer. Gio+ reads it from the camera image, with the iPhone sensor or with the Apple Watch. You can also take it from a photo or enter it by hand.

A · Camera

Measure with the camera

The angle arc and the degrees are drawn right on the camera image, like on a goniometer.

B · Sensor

Measure with the iPhone sensor

Hold the iPhone against your body and its motion sensor measures the angle. For 22 movements.

C · Apple Watch

Measure with Apple Watch

The watch on your wrist measures seven arm movements and sends the result to your iPhone.

D · Photo

Read the angle from a photo

Gio+ finds the angle in a photo. You can move the three points or set them yourself.

E · Movements

38 movements in 10 body regions

From the cervical spine to the great toe. Every movement keeps its own history, separately for left and right.

Cervical spine3 Trunk3 Shoulder5 Elbow1 Forearm1 Wrist2 Hand and fingers15 Hip4 Knee1 Ankle and foot3
F · Voice guidance

Voice prompts guide the measurement

You stand 1 to 2 m from the device. Gio+ tells you what comes next and reads out each value.

“Extension 43 degrees. Now bend and hold.”
“Done. Average extension 43 degrees, flexion 101 degrees.”
G · History

History and weekly trend

A curve shows your range day by day. Pro adds the weekly trend and the photo history.

04 Who it is for

Injuries and operations where measuring pays off

After many injuries and operations the joint is stiff at first. Gio+ helps wherever flexion and extension are measured again and again over weeks.

Elbow

  • Elbow fracture: radial head, olecranon, distal humerus
  • Elbow dislocation
  • Ligament tear and ligament surgery
  • Stiff elbow after a cast or immobilisation
  • After arthrolysis

Knee

  • ACL tear and ACL reconstruction
  • Collateral ligament injury (MCL, LCL)
  • Meniscus tear and meniscus surgery
  • Knee replacement
  • Patella fracture, tibial plateau fracture
  • Arthrofibrosis

Shoulder

  • Frozen shoulder (adhesive capsulitis)
  • Rotator cuff tear and rotator cuff repair
  • Proximal humerus fracture
  • Shoulder dislocation
  • Shoulder replacement

Gio+ measures raising the arm to the front.

Notice

Gio+ also measures the cervical spine, trunk, forearm, wrist, hand and fingers, hip, and ankle and foot.

Gio+ only measures. How far you may move the joint is up to your doctor or physiotherapist, especially in the first weeks after an operation.

05 Privacy

Where your data is stored

Your measurements are health data. They stay on your devices and, if you want, in your private iCloud.

Local

On your device

Measurements, photos and profiles are stored on your device. Gio+ works offline.

Sync

In your iCloud

Your devices sync through your private iCloud. You can switch that off.

No server

No account, no server

Gio+ sends no data to any server. Camera images are analysed on the device.
