01 Measurement

Your iPhone as a goniometer

A physiotherapist reads joint angles off a goniometer. Gio+ reads them from the camera image. Your first measurement is the baseline, and every later one is compared with it.

  1. 01

    Set up your iPhone

    Lean it against something or use a stand and step back 1 to 2 m. A checklist confirms four things: the device is upright (at most 8° tilt), the joint is detected, you are side-on to the camera, and the distance fits. Once all four have been green for 1.5 s, the measurement starts by itself.

  2. 02

    Straighten and bend three times

    A value counts when you hold the joint at its limit for 2 s and it moves by no more than 3°. Voice prompts, sound and haptics tell you what comes next, so you do not have to look at the screen.

  3. 03

    Check the result

    Extension deficit, flexion and range of motion are saved as the average of the three rounds, with a photo of each end position. You can also enter a measurement by hand or from two photos; Gio+ detects the angle in the photo and lets you adjust it.

How far you are from the range everyday tasks need

Most everyday tasks need less than the full range of a healthy joint. Gio+ draws your current arc next to the functional and the normal range, so you can read off how many degrees are missing in flexion and in extension.

03 Features

Measurement, history and achievements

Your iPhone measures the angle. Gio+ stores it and compares it with your earlier measurements and with the functional range.

A · Camera

Measure with the camera

The overlay works like a goniometer: a dashed 0° line, the angle arc, the reading in degrees and a hold ring, drawn on the camera image. A live view shows the current angle without saving anything.

B · History

History and weekly trend

A curve shows range, flexion or extension deficit day by day, with the change since your first measurement. Pro adds the weekly trend and a photo history with comparison and time-lapse.

Weekly trend
C · Achievements

Milestones from daily life

Most achievements are tied to an everyday task: for the elbow, about 115° of flexion is enough to bring a spoon to your mouth. Others mark gains in range (+10°, +25°, +50°) and measuring streaks of 3 to 30 days.

04 Privacy

Where your data is stored

Range-of-motion values are health data. Gio+ keeps them in two places: on your iPhone and, if you leave sync on, in your private iCloud.

Local

On your device

Measurements, photos and profiles are stored in a database on your iPhone. Gio+ also works offline.

Sync

In your iCloud

Your devices sync through your private iCloud. You can switch that off in the settings at any time.

No server

No account, no server

Gio+ sends no data to its own servers or to anyone else's. Camera images are analysed on the device.

Details in the privacy policy.
